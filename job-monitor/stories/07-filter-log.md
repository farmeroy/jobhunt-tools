# Story 7: Filter Log & Review

**As a job seeker, I want to review what the tool found and what it filtered out**, so I can tune relevance and catch false negatives.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `job-monitor log` shows recent fetch results: new postings found, title filter results, content scores
- [ ] Can filter the log by company, date, or filter outcome (passed/rejected)
- [ ] Rejected postings are queryable — not silently discarded
- [ ] Enough detail to spot false negatives (e.g., a good role that got title-rejected)
